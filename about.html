About Destiny McCoy

Bold truth, healing-centered care, and practical support for anxiety.

I coach individuals and organizations to create, promote, and sustain wellness practices with a focused expertise in anxiety support. The goal is simple: help people feel grounded, informed, and empowered in their daily lives.

Why I do this work

I am a bold believer and anxiety annihilator. I share my own story openly and without shame, because transparency creates hope and gives people language for what they are navigating.

For more than a decade, I have built safe spaces for justice-involved individuals, youth, and urban communities. My approach centers women and communities often overlooked by traditional systems of care.

Impact in practice

  • Strategic consulting for wellness-focused organizations
  • Coaching that turns insight into realistic daily practices
  • Support that elevates confidence, sustainability, and growth
  • Culturally aware spaces where people feel seen and safe
